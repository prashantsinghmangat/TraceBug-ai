Recording

What a recording may use besides the screen.

Microphone Not granted

Only used when you turn it on for a recording.

It has to be granted here, not from the toolbar popup: a popup closes the moment the browser's permission prompt takes focus, which cancels the request. Nothing is recorded until you start a recording, and the audio stays in the report on your device.

Privacy

What TraceBug masks before anything leaves the page.

Redaction rules

App-specific PII masked at capture, on top of TraceBug's built-in token and secret patterns.

Upgrading from before 1.11?

Earlier versions stored tokens in each website's own storage, where scripts on that site could read them. Revoke and re-create any token you configured before 1.11, then enter the new one here.
