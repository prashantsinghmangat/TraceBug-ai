You found a bug. You ask your AI to fix it... and it asks you for the error, the steps, and the logs.
Meet TraceBug.
Install it free, on Chrome or Firefox. No account needed.
Click Capture, and reproduce the bug. Every click, console error and network request is recorded.
You get one report, with the error, the stack trace, a replay, and the video.
Click Fix with AI, and the whole report is copied as a ready-made prompt.
Paste it into Claude Code. It reads the error and the stack trace, and opens the right file.
It finds the real cause: SAVE20 was never added to the coupon list. And it fixes it.
Reload, apply the code, and the discount works.
